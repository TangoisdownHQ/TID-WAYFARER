A commercial carrier is a leg, not a marketplace participant. UPS will never run an outpost or sign a receipt, so the depot that shipped stays accountable: the custody chain records the handover with the tracking number as evidence and marks it attested rather than signed. Carrier cost is fiat; the marketplace leg still settles in TIDAT.
